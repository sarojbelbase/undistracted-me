Undistracted Me

A free, open-source new tab page for Chrome and Firefox — a calm, distraction-free dashboard with a Nepali calendar, a cinematic Focus Mode, and 30+ quiet widgets. No account, works offline.

What is Undistracted Me?

Undistracted Me replaces the noisy default new tab with a quiet dashboard designed for deep, focused work. Instead of shortcuts, news feeds, and clutter, you get a clean surface you control — arrange the widgets you actually want and hide the rest.

Features

Focus Mode

Focus Mode turns your new tab into a full-screen, cinematic clock. Pick a time display and an ambient background, then let the screen settle into a distraction-free view while you work.

Dashboard widgets

The Dashboard is a draggable grid of 30+ widgets: weather, air quality, Nepali stock indices, currency and gold rates, RSS feeds, notes, tasks, a Pomodoro timer, and more. Rearrange, resize, and toggle each one to build the layout that fits your day.

Nepali calendar

The dashboard is built around a Nepali calendar with both Bikram Sambat and Gregorian dates, today's tithi and events, and the year's public holidays — all computed offline.
