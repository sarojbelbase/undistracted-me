## 4. The "no Nepali calendar in visible text" rule — decision needed

**Status: standing decision (you chose "keep the rule").** This is the single
biggest lever for AI + Google visibility, because you are currently invisible
for "nepali date today" / "aaja kati gate" / "nepali calendar" in body copy.

Options, in order of impact:

1. **Lift the rule** and write "Nepali calendar" in visible body copy.
2. **Scoped exception** (recommended): allow the phrase only on the `/compare/`
   pages and one dedicated "Nepali date" section, keeping the homepage clean.
3. Keep the rule and stay machine-only for those queries (current state).
